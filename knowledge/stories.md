# Customer stories

These are the only customer stories Sales Navigator may use. Every story comes from the InfluxData [Customers](https://www.influxdata.com/customers/), [Partners](https://www.influxdata.com/partners/) or [Blog](https://www.influxdata.com/blog/) pages. Each one was checked against its page in September 2026.

Stories that say which product the company replaced (for example "Replaced Graphite") are also listed as **Switch stories** in `competitors.md`.

To add a story, add a row. Copy the company name, the headline result and the link straight from the InfluxData page. Don't round or improve the numbers.
